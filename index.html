Author’s Note

A donut, like ourselves, is a torus: a form defined by the space it encircles.

Its body and its center are not two separate things.

Emptiness and fullness, arriving from opposite directions: the Buddhist Heart Sutra and the Hindu Upanishads.

This is an invitation to notice, through a little book about a donut that is really about everything.

Hold it in your hands.

Donut is becoming a book. Leave your email and you will hear the day it is ready. You can also ask for a print of any painting.
